Splitting the name in two weights is the cheapest, most durable move here: Střední states the category, na míru states the difference, and the measuring notation makes "tailored" literal without a single icon. Also the only direction that survives being read aloud.
Risk: gives you no avatar. A logotype cannot be a 40px email picture — this would have to pair with a mark from elsewhere in the set, which is normal, but it means picking twice.
