Why this one
The háček is a piece of Czech writing no other market has — it reads as native, not as a Czech translation of an American product. Symmetric on purpose: a checkmark is lopsided, a háček is not, which is what stops this reading as a generic UI tick. It also sits over the ř in Střední, so the mark and the name are literally the same object.
Risk: at a glance some people will still see a checkmark. Defensible — but it is the one thing to pressure-test with a student before committing.
