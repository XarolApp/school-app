Střední na míru
Takes the name literally — na míru is bespoke tailoring, and this is the mark a tailor actually cuts. Adult, quiet, and completely absent from Czech edtech.
Risk: the tailoring reference is invisible unless someone explains it. Students may read it as an abstract shape and nothing more.
