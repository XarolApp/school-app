The most product-true mark in either round: every Czech 9th grader submits exactly three schools in priority order, and this is that form. Descending widths read as a ranking without needing numbers.
Risk: at a glance it can read as a generic list or a signal-strength meter. The three-tint scale is what saves it — so it must never be flattened to one colour in mono.
