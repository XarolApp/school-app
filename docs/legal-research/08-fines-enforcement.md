# 8. Fines and enforcement: ČOI and ÚOOÚ

> Research as of 21. 9. 2026. Not legal advice.

> Historical enforcement statistics below have not been refreshed or independently recalculated in the October review. They are not a prediction, a compliance budget or evidence that an individual operator will receive a small fine. Current engineering/legal gates are in the [deployment report](../../reports/deployment-review-2026-10-07/REPORT.md).

## ČOI (consumer law)
- **2024 totals:** 21 149 inspections, violations in **41.33 %**, **7 334 fines**, **89 340 400 Kč** total → **average ≈ 12 180 Kč**. — [ČOI výroční zpráva 2024](https://coi.gov.cz/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/COI-vyrocni-zprava-2024.pdf)
- E-shops: ~80 % of 371 checked had violations; most common = missing ADR info, missing complaint procedure, unfair practices. — [ČOI](https://coi.gov.cz/s-rozvojem-internetove-obchodovani-vzrostly-pocty-poruseni-zakonu/)
- **Maximum**: up to 5 000 000 Kč under zákon 634/1992 Sb. (secondary sources; verify §24). Withdrawal button breaches fall here from 2027.
- §1826a button breach → **contract not concluded** (civil consequence, not a fine).
- No reliable individual penalty estimate was established by this research.

## ÚOOÚ (data protection, cookies)
- Cookie enforcement: 120+ warning letters first, then **4 443 000 Kč** in fines Jan–Jul 2023; biggest **898 000 Kč** (marketing cookies without consent). — [ÚOOÚ](https://uoou.gov.cz/media-publikace/tiskove-zpravy/urad-udelil-pokuty-za-45-mil-kc)
- 2022 monitoring: contacted operators first, fines if not fixed. — [ÚOOÚ](https://uoou.gov.cz/cs/cookies-listy-vykazuji-radu-nedostatku)
- Statutory maxima do not predict an individual outcome; operator size alone is not immunity.

## DSA
- [Parliament print 69](https://www.psp.cz/sqw/historie.sqw?o=10&T=69) remained pending after second reading on 8 October 2026. This alone does not establish the complete existing enforcement/fine regime. Do not reuse the earlier "no Czech fine regime" assertion without a current legal check.

## Your realistic risk
The ranking of likely complaints was speculative. Current source has operator name/address/contact and no payment checkbox, while business-status verification, minor-contract evidence, beta device storage, durable confirmations and reliable refunds remain open. Correct those substantiated gaps and obtain the relevant legal decisions; the historical statistics do not justify accepting a defect.
